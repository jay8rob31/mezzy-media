About Mezzy Media

Mezzy Media is an Atlanta-based digital news and culture brand. Our mission is simple: be first with the news, most credible on the facts, and most entertaining on the culture. We cover the stories that shape daily life in metro Atlanta — from public safety to the food, sports, and nightlife that make the city what it is — and deliver them in a voice that actually sounds like Atlanta.

AI & editorial disclosure. Mezzy Media's stories are produced with an AI-assisted newsroom. Our named personas (Aaliyah, Jamal, Priya, Xavier and others) are editorial voices for our desks, not real individuals. Public-safety, case-update and mission coverage is kept strictly factual regardless of topic.
